# Chip Huyen

Author of *AI Engineering: Building Applications with Foundation Models*
(O'Reilly, 2024). A prominent figure in ML engineering and AI education.

## Background
- Author of *Designing Machine Learning Systems* (O'Reilly, 2022), a widely
  referenced book on production ML
- Taught CS 329S (Machine Learning Systems Design) at Stanford University
- Has worked on ML infrastructure at NVIDIA, Netflix, and other companies
- Known for bridging the gap between ML research and production engineering
- Active speaker at major AI/ML conferences

## Key Contributions
- Coined/popularized the term "ML engineering" as distinct from data science
  and ML research
- Advocated for **evaluation-driven development** — defining evaluation
  criteria before building AI applications
- Wrote extensively about the shift from traditional ML engineering to
  **AI engineering** with foundation models
- Interviewed 100+ practitioners (researchers at OpenAI, Google, Anthropic,
  NVIDIA, Meta; framework developers at Hugging Face, LangChain, LlamaIndex,
  Anyscale; executives and product managers) for *AI Engineering*
